FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

The setup is free. Running costs are $0 because the bot uses free tiers: one small Google Cloud server, the YouTube Data API and Google's Gemini AI. Google needs a card on file to activate the free server, but stays at $0 within the free limits, and I set a tiny budget alert so you'd hear about any charge.

What do I need?

A YouTube channel that streams, a Google account for the bot's server, and a second YouTube channel for the bot itself that you make a moderator on your channel. Optional: a Discord server for reports and Shorts ideas.

Is it safe for my channel?

It uses only the official YouTube API — no scraping, no fake viewers. It can only delete messages and give short timeouts (5 minutes max); it has no ban tool and can never act on you or your mods. Every AI reply is checked before posting.

Which languages does it speak?

Whatever your chat speaks — English, Hindi, Hinglish, Urdu, or a mix. The personality is written around your channel.

Who owns the bot and the data?

You do. Everything runs in your own Google account, and chat data stays there. You can switch it off any time.
